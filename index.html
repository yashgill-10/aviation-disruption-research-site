Research Question and Scope

How do Flight/Weather and Airport/Network scores compare in ranking and classifying BTS departure delays at T−2h and T−30m on common 2024 flights, and how does a logistic stack of their continuous probabilities compare in the November–December exploratory evaluation?

This is a matched comparison of different pipelines, not a controlled feature ablation. Estimator families, training populations, feature engineering, and threshold selection periods differ across models.

Data, Identity, and Eligibility

The cleaned 2024 source contains 6,982,766 data rows. Canonical identity resolves 6,982,525 rows uniquely; 220 rows are ambiguous, 1 is identity-incomplete, and 20 are unmatched. These exceptions were preserved rather than assigned inferred IDs.

Retrospective eligibility rules use final BTS outcomes: T−2h requires CANCELLED == 0 and DEP_DELAY > -120 minutes; T−30m requires CANCELLED == 0 and DEP_DELAY > -30 minutes.

39 ambiguous October–December rows were scored but excluded from canonical matched comparison due to no defensible canonical ID.

Model Methodology and Live Inference Status

Flight/Weather Airport/Network M3 Stack

Flight/Weather Models

Training: SGDClassifier trained on 2022–2023. Platt LogisticRegression calibrator and F1 threshold selected on January–June 2024. September scores used saved classifier and calibrator without refitting.

Features: Schedule fields, historical delay rates (prior 3 months, 7/30 days), and GHCNh FM15 METAR observations with 15-minute availability proxy and 180-minute staleness limit.

Live Inference Status: Historical evaluation complete. Local inference prototype exists via src/flight_weather_web.py and src/predict_current_flights.py for prediction-time schedule data with prior BTS history and available decoded METARs.

Limitations: Complete estimator hyperparameter, feature-selection, and model-family selection history not recovered. Weather uses observation-time proxy, not actual receipt timestamps. Schedule-vintage and BTS publication timestamps unavailable.

Airport/Network Models

Training: RandomForestClassifier (50 trees, max depth 12, min leaf size 10, class_weight="balanced_subsample", random state 42). Stratified sample up to 200,000 eligible January–August 2024 flights per horizon. September used to choose operating threshold by maximizing F1.

Features: Airport network state, recent activity reconstructed from final BTS outcomes, schedule information.

Live Inference Status: Historical evaluation complete. Live inference is not validated; original scoring-time feature builder not recovered.

Limitations: Original scoring-time feature builder not recovered. Recorded builder SHA-256 a77c70db... identifies unrecovered source; commonly cited c107a5eb... is the scoring runner, not the builder. Complete model selection history not recovered.

M3 Logistic Stack

Architecture: Logistic Regression (C=1, solver="lbfgs", max_iter=1000) on two logit-transformed, standardized continuous component probabilities.

Protocol: September 2024 meta-training, October 2024 threshold selection (maximize F1), November–December 2024 evaluation. StandardScaler fit on September rows only. Probabilities clipped to [1e-6, 1−1e-6] before logit transformation.

Live Inference Status: Retrospective stacking evaluation complete. Live inference is not validated; requires both Flight/Weather and Airport/Network component scores for new predictions.

Limitations: Exact row-level September training-ID list not saved. M3 model files serialized after evaluation predictions calculated; pre-evaluation serialized-model hash not established.

Threats to Validity and Limitations

  • Provenance: Airport/Network original scoring-time builder not recovered. M3 row-level September training-ID list not saved. Complete model selection histories for all three models not recovered.
  • Feature availability: Schedule-vintage timestamps, BTS publication/ingestion times, and recent airport-state availability timestamps unavailable. Flight/Weather uses 15-minute weather observation-time proxy, not actual receipt timestamps.
  • Evaluation status: October–December 2024 and 2025 have been inspected; neither is a blind final test. 2025 cannot serve as an untouched holdout.
  • Threshold differences: Component thresholds selected in different periods (Flight/Weather Jan–Jun, Airport/Network Sep); M3 threshold selected October. Procedures not identical.
  • Matched comparison only: Different pipelines compared on matched IDs; differences cannot be attributed exclusively to feature additions.
  • Live inference: Flight/Weather has a local inference prototype; Airport/Network and M3 live inference are not validated. This website displays stored results only.
  • No deployment claims: Results do not establish real-time feature availability, operational readiness, causal effects, or deployment validity.

Conclusion

The saved retrospective results show that the M3 logistic stack ranked delayed flights better by average precision and captured more delayed flights by recall than either component on the November–December 2024 matched sample. Precision was lower, and threshold-dependent results varied by horizon. These findings are exploratory, not a blind final-test result. Builder provenance, feature availability, selection history, and a genuinely untouched test period remain unresolved. No live-readiness or passenger-service claim is made.
